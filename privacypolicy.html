Privacy policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

HYPE is an iOS app that tracks upcoming and newly released movies, TV shows, music and games. It is built and run by Daniel Fisher. This page explains exactly what the app collects, why, and how to get rid of it.

The short version

HYPE collects your name and email from Sign in with Apple, the titles you favourite, and a notification token if you turn alerts on. That is all. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking across other apps or websites, and nothing is ever sold.

What we collect

What HYPE collects and why
WhatWhy we need it
Your name and email addressSupplied by Sign in with Apple when you create an account, so we can identify you. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we only ever see the relay address.
An account IDLinks your account to your own list.
Titles you favourite, and your feed preferencesSo your list syncs across your devices and the feed shows the media you care about.
A notification tokenOnly if you allow notifications. It lets us send the release-day alert for the titles you follow.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics or usage tracking. The app ships without any analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • No location, contacts, photos, calendar or health data.
  • No payment details. HYPE is free and has no purchases.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, to keep your favourites in step across your devices, to send the alerts you asked for, and to show the media types you have chosen. We do not sell your information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not build a profile of you.

Where your data is stored

Your account and favourites are stored on Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database, Cloud Storage and Cloud Messaging), which processes the data on our behalf under Google's terms. Nobody else has access.

Where the titles come from

Release dates, artwork, scores and store links are fetched from third-party sources — including TMDB, IGDB, AlbumOfTheYear, SteamGridDB, Apple, Spotify and YouTube. These are lookups about films, shows, albums and games. Your personal information is never sent to them.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app: open your profile and choose Delete account. This removes your account, your favourites and your notification token. If you would rather we did it for you, email hypeappfeedback@gmail.com and we will action it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Delete the account and it goes. Backups holding the data are rotated out within 30 days.

Children

HYPE is not aimed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us their details, email us and we will delete them.

Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to erase it. Email hypeappfeedback@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update this page and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be flagged in the app.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else: hypeappfeedback@gmail.com
